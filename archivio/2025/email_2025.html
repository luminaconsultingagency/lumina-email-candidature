Dear Student,
We are excited to introduce Lumina , the consulting agency founded in 2023 and entirely managed by H-FARM College students, with the support of Professor Raffaele Testorelli.

Our mission is clear: to transform theory into practice , offering the opportunity to work on real projects with leading companies and build concrete connections with the professional world.
The companies we have worked with
Over the past year, we have already collaborated with companies such as Allianz Bank, Electrolux, Nakheel, Kion, Generali, SKF, and many others, bringing new perspectives on Generation Z to businesses and growing ourselves through a constant exchange of ideas and value.
How We Are Organized.
Lumina is organized into Units , dedicated to managing external projects with companies and partners, and Internal Teams , which deal with internal life and development.
